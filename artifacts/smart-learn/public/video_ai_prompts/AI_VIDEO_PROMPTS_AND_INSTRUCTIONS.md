```text
   - The glowing "MN" badge floats center screen.
   - Text appears: "Expert Moodle LMS & AI Architect".
   - Three glowing statistics glass cards smoothly slide in from below: "100,000+ Active Users Managed", "70+ LMS Projects Done", and "2020–2025 Particularly Helpful Badge on Moodle.org".

2. (0:15 - 0:30) QUIZ AI CHAT TUTOR (local_qai):

   - A glowing emerald badge pops up: "92 / 100 · A+ (Graded Automatically in Seconds)".

4. (0:45 - 1:00) KASHIER PAYMENT GATEWAY (paygw_kashier):
   - A checkout modal appears showing "#1 Moodle Payment Gateway for Egypt & MENA".
   - Payment method badges light up: "Fawry Pay", "Meeza Card", "Visa / MC".
   - An emerald banner slides in: "✓ Instant Course Enrollment Confirmed".

5. (1:00 - 1:15) ENTERPRISE n8n AUTOMATION & RAG SEARCH + CTA:

   - Text zooms in gently: "Ready to Upgrade Your LMS Platform? Let's build together."

CAMERA & MOTION:
- Silky smooth easing between scenes, no abrupt jump cuts.
- Crisp typography in Cairo / Inter fonts.
- Professional Apple-keynote or stripe-level motion graphics quality.
```
